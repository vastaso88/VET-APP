# VetApp Privacy Policy (Australia)

Version: en-AU v1 (draft). Last updated: [dd/mm/yyyy].

This policy explains how we handle your personal information when you use VetApp in Australia. We aim to follow the Australian Privacy Principles (APPs) in the *Privacy Act 1988* (Cth), even where the law does not strictly require us to.

## 1. Who we are

[Company name], ABN [number], [registered address]. Privacy contact: [privacy email address]. You can write to this address about anything in this policy.

## 2. What we collect and where it comes from

| What | Examples | Where it comes from |
|---|---|---|
| Account details | Email, name, date you signed up, the choices you made about consents (with date and version) | You, when you sign up. Your password is managed by our sign-in provider |
| Your pets | Name, species, breed, age, weight, notes, photos and videos | You |
| Documents and health records | Documents you upload and health events you record for a pet. They may contain information about you or other people (for example a name or address on a vet invoice) | You |
| Chat | Your questions, the replies, attachments, voice messages (turned into text so we can answer) | You and our system |
| Location | GPS location, only if you allow it on your device, or an address you type in. For walks, the route: GPS points with date, time and accuracy, linked to your account and your pet | You, through your device |
| Contributions | Place reports, star ratings for dog parks and votes. These are linked to a pseudonym, not your name | You |
| Technical data | IP address and server logs | Our system, for operation and security |
| Subscription | The plan you picked, the start and end of your free trial. Payments are handled by the Apple App Store or Google Play | You, and Apple or Google |

We do not knowingly collect information from people under 18. VetApp is for adults.

**Device permissions.** The app asks for a permission (location, microphone, notifications, camera or photos) only when you use the feature that needs it. If you say no, that feature stays off and the rest of the app keeps working.

## 3. Why we collect it, and how we use it

- To create and run your account and provide the service: pets, reminders, chat, walks.
- To answer your questions in the chat, using an external artificial intelligence (AI) provider (see section 7).
- To let the chat read a pet's health record, **only if you turn that on for that pet**. You can turn it off at any time.
- To show nearby places and collect place reports and ratings.
- To send you marketing emails, **only if you opt in**. You can opt out at any time.
- To measure how the app is used, **only if you opt in** [confirm whether any analytics tool is actually active; if not, delete this line].
- To keep the service secure, prevent abuse and meet our legal duties.

We use your information for these purposes, or for a related purpose you would reasonably expect. Otherwise we ask for your consent, unless the law allows or requires it.

## 4. Marketing

If you opt in, we may email you news about VetApp and tips for your pets. Every marketing email has an unsubscribe link that works without logging in. We act on an unsubscribe request within five business days. You can also withdraw your consent in Settings. Saying no does not affect your use of the app.

## 7. How we use AI

The replies in the chat are written by an AI system and are marked as AI-generated. They can be wrong. Before your text goes to the AI provider, our system removes some recognisable personal details, such as emails, phone numbers and street addresses. It cannot catch everything (for example, a person's name written in the middle of a sentence). Images and audio are sent as they are. [Reviewer: see note 3.]

If you give consent for a pet, the chat can read that pet's most recent health records and send them to the AI provider. VetApp uses that only to answer you.

**Automated decisions.** We do not use automated systems to make decisions that significantly affect your legal rights or interests. [Reviewer: from 10/12/2026 APP 1 requires a statement about automated decisions; see note 5.]
