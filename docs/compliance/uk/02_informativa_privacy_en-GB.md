## 2. What personal information we use

| Category | Examples | Where it comes from |
|---|---|---|
| Account | Email address, first and last name, date you registered, your consent choices with date and version, and the language and region version you were shown. Optional: phone number (no feature uses it; it stays in your profile and you can change or remove it) and profile photo (only you can see it, in a private area of your account; you can change or remove it). The phone number and photo are deleted with your account | You, when you sign up or from your profile. Your password is handled by our sign-in provider |
| Your pets | Name, species, breed, age, weight, notes, photos and videos | You |
| Documents and health record | Documents you upload and health events you record. These may contain your own details or other people's (for example, your name or address on a vet bill) | You |
| Chat | Your questions, the assistant's answers, attachments and voice messages (which we turn into text to answer you) | You and the system |
| Location | Your GPS position, only if you allow it on your device, or an address you type in. For walks, the route: GPS points with date, time and accuracy, linked to your account and pet | You, through your device |
| Contributions | Place reports, star ratings and votes. They are linked to a pseudonym, not your name | You |
| Marketplace | Listings, photos, prices and the area you choose | You |
| Technical data | IP address and server logs | The system, to run the service and keep it secure |
| Subscription | The plan you choose, the start and end of your free trial. We do not hold card details: payments are [not yet active / handled by (payment provider)] | You and the system |

We do not knowingly ask for special category data (such as health data about you). A document you upload could contain some. Please do not upload documents about your own health.

### Device permissions

The app asks for a permission only when you use a feature that needs it. If you say no, that feature stays off and the rest of the app keeps working.

| Permission | When we ask | What we receive |
|---|---|---|
| Location while you use the app | To set your area, see nearby places, record walks | The coordinates your device gives us. While a walk is running, the app keeps recording your position even when it is in the background, and a visible notification tells you |
| Microphone | To record a voice message for the chat | The recording, which is turned into text [provider: to be confirmed] |
| Notifications | To remind you about vaccinations and treatments | No data: it only shows alerts |
| Photos, videos and files | To add pet photos and attach documents | Only the files you choose, through the system picker |

## 6. How we use AI

Chat answers are written by an AI system and are marked as AI-generated. Before sending text to the provider we automatically remove some recognisable personal details, such as emails, phone numbers and addresses with a house number. This cannot catch everything, for example a person's name inside a sentence. Images and audio are sent as they are. [Reviewer note: do not say the health record is filtered until the issue in register item 28 is fixed.]

The assistant gives general information. It does not diagnose, prescribe or replace a veterinary surgeon. See our AI Notice.

We do not make decisions about you that have legal or similarly significant effects based solely on automated processing.
